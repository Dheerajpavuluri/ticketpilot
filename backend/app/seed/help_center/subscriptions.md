---
title: Managing your coffee subscription
category: subscription
---
You can pause, skip, change or cancel your subscription anytime under Account → Subscriptions. Changes made at least 2 days before your next ship date apply to that shipment.

- Skip: skips only the next delivery.
- Pause: stops deliveries for 1–3 months.
- Cancel: stops all future deliveries. There are no cancellation fees.

If you were charged for a shipment after canceling, contact us and we'll refund it.
