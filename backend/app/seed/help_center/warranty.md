---
title: Warranty coverage
category: product_issue
---
Espresso machines carry a 2-year limited warranty. Grinders and kettles carry a 1-year limited warranty. The warranty starts on the delivery date.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as pump failure, heating element failure and electrical faults. It doesn't cover scale buildup, damage from dropping the unit, or use with non-filtered hard water.

To start a claim, send your order number, a short description of the problem and, if you can, a video. We'll troubleshoot with you first; if the unit is defective we'll repair or replace it.
