---
title: How to descale your espresso machine
category: product_issue
---
Descale every 2–3 months, or monthly if you have hard water. Signs you need to descale: slower flow, lower temperature, or a "descale" light.

1. Mix one packet of Brewline descaler with 1 liter of water in the reservoir.
2. Run half the solution through the group head and half through the steam wand.
3. Let the machine sit for 20 minutes.
4. Rinse by running two full reservoirs of fresh water through.

Skipping descaling is the most common cause of weak pressure and is not covered by warranty.
