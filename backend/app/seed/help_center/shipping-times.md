---
title: Shipping times and carriers
category: shipping
---
Orders placed before 1 PM Pacific on a business day ship the same day. Later orders ship the next business day.

Standard shipping (free on orders over $50) takes 3–5 business days within the contiguous US. Expedited shipping takes 1–2 business days and costs $14.95.

We ship with UPS and USPS. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as the label is created. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

Alaska, Hawaii and US territories add 2–4 business days. We don't currently ship internationally.
