---
title: Returns and refunds
category: returns_refunds
---
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.

- Machines, grinders and accessories must be unused and in the original packaging.
- Opened coffee beans can't be returned for food safety reasons. If a bag tastes off, contact us and we'll replace it.
- Return shipping is free for defective items. For change-of-mind returns we deduct a $9.95 return label fee.

Refunds are issued within 3 business days after the return arrives at our warehouse. Your bank may take another 5–10 days to post it.

Refunds over $100 are reviewed by our support team before they're issued.
