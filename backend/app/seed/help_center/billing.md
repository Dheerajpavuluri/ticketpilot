---
title: Payments and billing
category: billing
---
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal and Apple Pay.

When you place an order, your bank may show a temporary authorization. It drops off within 3–5 business days and isn't a second charge.

If you see two completed charges for the same order, contact us with the order number and the last four digits of your card. We'll confirm and refund the duplicate.

For chargebacks or payment disputes already opened with your bank, our billing team handles the case directly.
