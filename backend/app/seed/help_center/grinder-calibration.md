---
title: Dialing in your grinder
category: product_issue
---
If espresso shots run too fast (under 20 seconds) and taste sour, grind finer. If they run too slow (over 35 seconds) and taste bitter, grind coarser.

Move one step at a time and purge about 2 grams after each change. New grinders often need 1–2 lb of beans to break in, so settings drift a little in the first weeks.

A grinding noise without beans coming out usually means a jammed burr. Unplug the grinder, remove the hopper and clear the burr chamber with the included brush.
