---
title: Tracking your order
category: shipping
---
Every shipped order has a tracking number. You can find it in your shipping confirmation email or under Account → Orders.

If tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days, or it shows "delivered" but you can't find the package, contact us with your order number. We'll open a trace with the carrier and, if the package is lost, send a replacement at no cost.

Please check with neighbors and around your property first; carriers sometimes mark a package delivered a few hours before it arrives.
