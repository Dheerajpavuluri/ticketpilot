---
title: Account and password help
category: account
---
To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link expires after 1 hour.

If you don't get the email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder. You can update your email address, saved addresses and payment methods under Account → Settings.

To delete your account and personal data, contact us from the email address on the account. Deletion is processed within 30 days.
